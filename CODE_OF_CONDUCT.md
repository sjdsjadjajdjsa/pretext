# Code of conduct

## Expected

- Assume good faith. Most disagreements here are about prompt wording or schema
  shape, not competence.
- Critique the change, not the person.
- Make room for people newer to Python, Typer, or red-team work. Explain the why.
- Keep review turnaround reasonable; a stalled PR should get a note, not silence.

## Not allowed

- Harassment, gatekeeping, or dismissiveness in issues and reviews.
- Pasted output containing secrets, customer names, or personal data.
- Claiming a generated pretext is verified when it has not been checked.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, label, or close contributions that ignore this document.
Repeat problems lead to a block. Escalate by opening an issue mentioning a
maintainer.

## Scope

Applies to the repository, its issues, its pull requests, and any project chat.
